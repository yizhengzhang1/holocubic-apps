黑底发光动态时钟

Luminous animated clock

烟花时钟

Fireworks Clock

NTP 数字时钟与实时烟花粒子同屏呈现，专为 HoloCubic 分光棱镜设计。

An NTP digital clock with real-time fireworks, tuned for the HoloCubic prism display.

功能与操作

Features & controls

  • 1
    十种烟花

    所有升空火箭和爆炸火丝都有前粗后细、前亮后暗的渐变尾迹，并包含金色瀑布、锦冠、彗星长尾、五发连珠和多次子爆。

  • 2
    整点烟花秀

    每个整点自动进行五种不同烟花齐射，并用机身 RGB 灯同步闪光。

  • 3
    随手互动

    短按任意方向键放一发；快速晃动机身触发三发齐射。

  • 4
    自动校时

    使用设备时区和阿里云 NTP，显示本地时间、日期与星期。

  • 1
    Ten firework styles

    Every rising shell and burst spark has a tapered brightness trail, including golden waterfall, brocade crown, comet, five-shot volley and multibreak effects.

  • 2
    Hourly showcase

    Five different effects launch at every full hour, with a synchronized RGB LED flash.

  • 3
    Physical interaction

    Short-press any direction key for one rocket, or shake the cube for a three-shot volley.

  • 4
    Automatic time sync

    Uses the device timezone and Aliyun NTP for local time, date and weekday.

烟花图鉴

Firework gallery

十种动图均由 HoloCubic 设备实时渲染并逐帧录制，完整展示升空、爆开、垂落与渐隐过程。

All ten animations were rendered and recorded frame by frame on the HoloCubic device, showing ascent, burst, fall and fade.

牡丹Peony

内外层带着清晰渐变尾迹均匀放射，形成饱满的圆形花冠。

Layered radial sparks carry clear tapered trails into a full, rounded bloom.

菊花Chrysanthemum

双层密集圆壳同步展开，轮廓更大、更厚实。

Two dense circular layers open into a large, full shell.

垂柳Willow

金色火丝以近端粗亮、远端细暗的渐变长尾缓缓下垂，像夜空中的柳枝。

Golden trails taper from a thick, bright head to a fine, dim tail as they fall like willow branches.

银金彗星Comet

一颗高亮彗星头持续升空，拖着密集、由白转金并逐渐变细变暗的长尾；顶端紧凑余辉稍作停留后渐暗消失，不再向两侧分裂。

One brilliant comet rises behind a dense white-to-gold tail, ending in a compact glow that lingers briefly and fades smoothly.

旋升花火Spinner

略小于彗星的三层银白亮核沿 0.5–1 像素超窄幅、加粗长尾多圈旋升，到达高处后再绽放双层星芒。

A three-layer silver-white head, slightly smaller than the comet, follows a long 0.5–1 px corkscrew before opening into a two-layer starburst.

频闪Strobe

亮点拖着稍长一些的短渐变尾迹间歇闪烁，呈现跳动的星芒效果。

Pulsing points keep slightly longer short tapered trails while flickering like stars.

五发连珠Rapidfire

五枚轻型花火从左到右连续升空，按固定节拍依次爆开。

Five light shells sweep left to right and burst in quick succession.

子母连爆Multibreak

主爆后四枚亮点飞出，再依次完成四次子爆。

Four bright carriers fly outward and burst in sequence.

金色瀑布Golden Waterfall

五个高低错落的小爆点同时展开，密集白金亮头收束成多簇近乎竖直、持续垂落的长金丝。

Five staggered burst points release dense white-gold heads into clustered, nearly vertical curtains of long golden strands.

锦冠Brocade Crown

完整圆周的白金亮点外密内疏地放射，拖着长金丝，后半程在重力下缓慢弯成饱满锦冠。

Dense white-gold points fill a complete circle, drawing long golden threads that slowly bend into a full brocade crown.
